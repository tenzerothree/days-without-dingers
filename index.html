Days Without Dinger

Why does this exist?

Vladimir Guerrero Jr. has not hit a home run since May 17, 2026, a streak now stretching over three weeks and counting.

The Blue Jays' offence tends to live and die with Vladdy's power. When he's not going deep, the whole lineup feels quieter, and so does Rogers Centre. This app is my way of watching that drought unfold in real time.

I figured others might want to track their own player's homerless misery too. So here we are. Enjoy?!

Code is available at github.com/tenzerothree/days-without-dinger. Feedback welcome via TENZEROTHREE.com.
